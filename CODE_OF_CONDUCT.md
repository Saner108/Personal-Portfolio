# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

To support a welcoming environment, contributors should:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent
- Give actionable feedback
- Focus discussion on the work, not individuals

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hateful language, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting comments, or disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, or pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct.
